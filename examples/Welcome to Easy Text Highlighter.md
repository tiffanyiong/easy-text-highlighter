> [!tip] A softer place to think
> Keep the words that make you pause. Leave the original note untouched.

## Small discoveries

A good idea deserves a little color. Select a passage, choose your pen, and keep reading.

Use a highlighter for the words you want to remember.
Use a wavy underline for a question worth returning to.
Use a straight underline for a detail you want to find again.

## Make room for a little wonder

Your highlights stay beside the note, organized under its nearest heading.

Five colors, three ways to mark a thought. Make the palette your own in Settings.

> [!note] Works with your favorite theme
> These screenshots use Animal Island Cozy. The plugin also works with the default Obsidian theme.

## Bring your ideas together

Filter by color or pen style, then export just the highlights you need.

1. Open Highlights from the ribbon.
2. Choose Export and select your excerpts.
3. Copy them or create a Markdown note.

No account. No network requests. Just your words, in your vault.
